Green-Agent Benchmark

A Text-Based Framework for Evaluating Perception and Planning Logic in Autonomous Driving Agents

🏎️ Team: Waymo Riders

Task Introduction

🎯

What is the Task?

▼

Our Green-Agent Benchmark evaluates autonomous driving AI agents on three critical capabilities:

  • Perception: Can the agent accurately identify objects, vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signals, and road features in driving scenes?
  • Prediction: Does the agent understand what's happening in the traffic situation and anticipate potential hazards?
  • Planning: Can the agent propose safe, legal, and appropriate driving actions for each scenario?

White-agents receive dashcam images and must provide text descriptions of what they observe and recommend optimal driving maneuvers.

🌍

What Does the Environment Look Like?

▼

Our evaluation environment consists of:

  • Image Dataset: Real-world dashcam images from KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite and publicly available traffic databases
  • Scenario Coverage: Common situations (lane following, intersections), edge cases (adverse weather, occlusions), and critical safety scenarios (pedestrian crossings, emergency stops)
  • Ground Truth Labels: Pre-annotated objects, positions, traffic conditions, and optimal responses created with human oversight
  • Traffic Rule Database: Comprehensive set of driving laws, safety regulations, and best practices for validation

The environment simulates real-world driving conditions through 2D visual inputs, making evaluation accessible while maintaining realism.

⚡

What Actions Can Agents Take?

▼

White-Agent Actions (Being Evaluated):

  • Provide text descriptions of perceived objects and their states
  • Describe traffic situations and potential hazards
  • Propose driving actions (accelerate, brake, turn, merge, stop, maintain speed)
  • Explain reasoning behind proposed actions

Green-Agent Actions (Evaluator):

  • Score perception accuracy by comparing descriptions to ground truth
  • Assess prediction quality based on hazard identification
  • Validate planning decisions against traffic laws and safety rules
  • Generate detailed reports with strengths, weaknesses, and failure analysis
  • Create leaderboards ranking agent performance

What the Green Agent Assesses

🔍

Perception Accuracy

▼

Our green agent evaluates how accurately white-agents identify and describe visual elements:

  • Object Detection: Correctly identifying vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, traffic signals, signs, and road features
  • Spatial Awareness: Accurately describing positions, distances, and relative locations
  • Completeness: Not missing critical objects or hazards in the scene
  • Precision: Providing specific details (colors, vehicle types, traffic light states) rather than vague descriptions

Scoring Method: Compare agent descriptions against ground truth annotations using semantic similarity and object-level matching.

🎯

Prediction & Situation Understanding

▼

Assessment of the agent's understanding of traffic dynamics and hazard recognition:

  • Hazard Identification: Recognizing potential dangers (pedestrians about to cross, vehicles braking, merge conflicts)
  • Intent Understanding: Predicting actions of other road users based on signals, positioning, and behavior
  • Situational Context: Understanding traffic flow, right-of-way rules, and environmental factors
  • Risk Assessment: Evaluating severity and likelihood of potential incidents

Scoring Method: Evaluate against expert annotations of critical hazards and expected traffic behaviors.

⚖️

Planning Validation & Safety

▼

Comprehensive evaluation of proposed driving actions:

  • Legal Compliance: Actions follow traffic laws, yield rules, speed limits, and signal regulations
  • Safety: Actions prioritize avoiding collisions and protecting vulnerable road users
  • Appropriateness: Actions are suitable for the specific scenario and not overly conservative or aggressive
  • Feasibility: Proposed maneuvers are physically executable given vehicle dynamics and constraints
  • Justification Quality: Reasoning demonstrates understanding of why the action is optimal

Scoring Method: Rule-based validation against traffic laws plus comparison to expert-labeled optimal actions.

📋

Explainability & Reporting

▼

Beyond numeric scores, our green agent generates actionable insights:

  • Failure Analysis: Specific situations where the agent made errors (e.g., "missed pedestrians in 3/10 crosswalk scenarios")
  • Strength Identification: Areas of consistent strong performance
  • Pattern Recognition: Common failure modes across multiple test cases
  • Improvement Recommendations: Concrete suggestions for enhancing agent capabilities
  • Comparative Analysis: How the agent performs relative to other agents and human baseline

This makes our benchmark uniquely valuable for iterative development and targeted improvements.

Design Notes: Test Case Selection

📚

How We Generated Test Cases

▼

Our test case generation follows a systematic approach:

  • Data Source Selection: Curated from KITTI benchmark (50+ scenarios) and real dashcam footage databases
  • Diversity Sampling: Stratified selection ensuring coverage of weather conditions, lighting, traffic density, and road types
  • Annotation Process: Expert human annotators label ground truth for objects, optimal actions, and hazards
  • Difficulty Calibration: Mix of easy, moderate, and challenging scenarios to ensure discriminative power
  • Edge Case Inclusion: Deliberately include rare but critical situations (occluded pedestrians, ambiguous right-of-way)
🎯

Why These Cases Test Reliability

▼

Our test suite ensures reliable evaluation through:

  • Reproducibility: Fixed dataset with deterministic ground truth ensures consistent scoring across runs
  • Coverage: Comprehensive scenarios (intersections, highways, urban, adverse weather) test all core capabilities
  • Discriminative Power: Difficulty range from obvious (clear road) to subtle (partially occluded pedestrian) separates agent skill levels
  • Real-World Relevance: Cases based on actual driving situations and common accident scenarios
  • Safety-Critical Focus: Heavy weight on scenarios where errors could cause accidents

This design ensures agents are tested on capabilities essential for safe autonomous driving.

✅

Success Criteria & Validation

▼

We validate our benchmark quality through:

  • Inter-Annotator Agreement: Multiple human experts annotate same scenarios; high agreement validates ground truth quality
  • Baseline Testing: Run simple rule-based agents and advanced AI models to verify score distribution
  • Consistency Checks: Verify same agent produces identical scores on repeated evaluations
  • Expert Review: Autonomous driving researchers validate that scenarios, labels, and scoring align with domain standards
  • Bias Detection: Analyze for systematic biases in test distribution or scoring that could advantage specific agent architectures

Impact & Future Directions

🚀

Real-World Applications

▼
  • Development Tool: Helps autonomous vehicle teams identify and fix perception, prediction, and planning weaknesses
  • Safety Validation: Provides standardized evaluation before deploying agents in real vehicles
  • Research Platform: Enables fair comparison of different AI approaches to autonomous driving
  • Educational Resource: Teaches students and engineers about critical aspects of self-driving AI
📈

Key Advantages

▼
  • Explainable: Unlike black-box numeric scores, provides detailed failure analysis and improvement recommendations
  • Comprehensive: Tests perception AND decision-making together, not just one capability
  • Accessible: Uses 2D images rather than requiring expensive simulation infrastructure
  • Realistic: Based on real driving scenarios and actual traffic laws
  • Reproducible: Standardized test suite ensures consistent evaluation
🔮

Future Enhancements

▼
  • Video Sequences: Extend from static images to temporal sequences testing dynamic prediction
  • Expanded Coverage: Add more geographic regions, traffic patterns, and cultural driving norms
  • Multi-Agent Scenarios: Test coordination and interaction between multiple autonomous vehicles
  • Uncertainty Quantification: Score agents on their ability to express confidence and acknowledge uncertainty
  • Human Baseline: Compare AI performance directly against human driver decisions

Making autonomous driving safer through comprehensive, explainable evaluation
